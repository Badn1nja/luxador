Why, right now

Lux: what Flint sees

The room's sensor is indoor: it measures daylight plus your lamps. From night-time history Flint learns what each lamp adds (so it can subtract its own light), and from daytime history how much daylight this room gets for a given sun height (used when the sensor drops out). Learned values only take effect when you press Apply learned; each one then shows (and can be locked) in the light's settings.

What goes together

Every signal as yes/no per 5 minutes. Goes with = usually true at the same time (× = how much more than at a random moment). Usually = hours it's on at least half the time. Now = how often it's on at this hour on this kind of day: the simplest honest prediction.
